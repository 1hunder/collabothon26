### StartDepot, youth products, demo conventions
- [web] StartDepot: "Das Depot für junge Leute"; no custody fees up to €20,000 depot volume (0.25% p.a. above); for pupils, students, trainees 0–29; from €25 in ETF/ETC/fund/share savings plans; 20% off online order commission. Under-18: guardians open in branch, both parents consent. https://www.commerzbank.de/privatkunden/investieren/depot/startdepot/
- [web] Savings-plan costs: ETF plan 0.50% + €2.50 per execution; share plan 1.5%. A free-purchase promotion appears to have ended 21.10.2026 — don't quote. https://www.commerzbank.de/privatkunden/investieren/wertpapier-sparplan/
- [web] Youth current account is "StartKonto" (free, from age 7, no overdraft, Girocard with Visa function). Prepaid Junior Kreditkarte from 14. No product named "Junges Konto" found. https://www.commerzbank.de/privatkunden/girokonten/startkonto/
- [web] Demo signalling: Stripe demo caution banner; Bank Simple demo mode with visible toggle; one distinctive banner style only. Third-party logo use needs explicit permission. No public Collabothon 2026 brand rules found.
- [web] Home-screen patterns: N26 Discreet Mode, Revolut hidden balance; Sparkasse home campaign banner leading into onboarding (children's depot example) — model for a game teaser card. Commerzbank has no dedicated hide-balance button (only per-account slider).
- [web] Pitfalls: every tappable element must go somewhere or show "not available in this prototype"; hi-fi look makes users assume everything is clickable; too many fake tiles distract from the entry point; a teaser that looks like an ad gets scrolled past; savings-plan confirmation must not read like a real order.
