## Design document findings (`Finance_Quest_Game_Loop_v2.pdf`, team-supplied → treated as [user])

- [user] **Governing principle:** "Roleplay determines how the player investigates. Historical market data determines what actually happened."
- [user] **Two loops:**
  - RPG action loop: DM presents a scene → player acts → Rules Engine resolves → DM narrates consequences.
  - Financial learning loop: Investigate → Decide → Commit → Reveal → Reflect → Progress.
- [user] **Resources:**
  - HP = virtual portfolio value / budget
  - XP = learning progress and reasoning quality
  - FOCUS = optional action/exploration resource
- [user] **Flowchart p.2, three layers:**
  - **01 Onboarding:** Character (load existing hero OR create new; choose class) → Quest selection (pick interest, virtual budget, time horizon) → Scenario Engine (load pre-checked anonymized historical data) → Dungeon Master (present scene, objective, known information).
  - **02 RPG action loop (repeat until the player commits):**
    - "DM: What would you do?" (player writes an action / asks the Buddy NPC) → Action router (free interaction / uncertain check / final decision).
    - Free/guaranteed interaction (read released data, request a definition, talk to Buddy) → straight to narration.
    - Uncertain RPG action (Rules Engine sets the DC; rolls D20 + modifier) → PASS / FAIL-FORWARD.
    - Both routes lead to "DM narrates consequences and updates the quest journal", then loop back ("NEW SCENE / NEW CLUE") to "What would you do?".
    - The router's COMMIT branch exits to layer 03.
  - **03 Financial learning loop (no dice-affected market outcomes):** Decision & forecast (action, evidence, risk, probability forecast) → Lock commitment (timestamp, no editing, hide future) → Historical outcome (engine reveals actual prices and returns) → HP update (portfolio value from market math) → Reflection and assessment (separate sound reasoning, prediction and luck) → XP, items, journal (score learning, unlock research tools/skills) → Next quest or finish (save persistent hero and results; start a fresh mission).
  - **Footer:** "RPG chance affects discovery and narrative cost — not historical returns. Essential financial information is never gated by luck."
- [user] **Adjudication (p.3):** Describe → Classify (free / uncertain / final commitment) → Resolve (free actions succeed without dice; only uncertain, meaningful actions roll; final decisions never roll) → Narrate (the journal records clues, resources, outcomes) → Repeat.
- [user] **Deterministic checks:**
  - Result = D20 + modifier. The Rules Engine, not the narrator, supplies the DC, modifiers, advantage/disadvantage and pass/fail.
  - DC 8: find a public filing → basic clue.
  - DC 12: unusual ratio → optional insight.
  - DC 15: cross-check conflicting evidence → stronger insight.
  - DC 18: obscure narrative lead → bonus clue/shortcut.
- [user] **Fail-forward:**
  - Success: bonus clue, faster route, or favourable NPC response.
  - Failure: a complication requiring another source.
  - Either way, essential evidence stays accessible.
  - No dice for viewing a chart, an ETF definition, required facts or computing returns.
  - A natural 20 can't make a bad investment profitable.
  - Class powers may reroll an investigation check, never returns.
- [user] **HP (p.4):**
  - 1 HP = €10; start 100 HP = €1,000.
  - Portfolio = cash + market value of holdings − explicit fees; HP = portfolio / €10.
  - Buying doesn't reduce HP.
  - Example: invest €400, keep €600, return −25% → €900 → 90 HP.
  - Fairness: every class gets the same investable budget in the MVP; the Fighter protects part as a cash reserve with no free capital. If starting HP varies, compare percentage outcomes.
- [user] **Classes (p.4):**
  - Wizard/Analyst "Arcane Insight": once per mission, reroll an investigation check.
  - Fighter/Guardian "Second Wind": restore 1 Focus.
  - Rogue/Investigator "Keen Eye": advantage on one clue check.
  - Cleric/Planner (optional) "Guidance": extra risk explanation.
- [user] **Resource systems:** HP changes only through recorded financial events; XP for reasoning, evidence, risk awareness, reflection, learning; Focus/items enable optional actions or unlock research tools, never extra investment power.
- [user] **Commit (p.5):** required inputs are Decision (invest / avoid / wait / diversify / other, with € amount), Evidence, Forecast (testable probability or interval with coverage), and Confidence (separate from the forecast). Lock gate: save, record the horizon, prevent revisions, then allow the reveal.
- [user] **Reveal:**
  - Numbers come from checked timestamped prices, not the LLM.
  - Portfolio display: initial capital, cash, holdings, fees, final value, absolute P&L and % return.
  - DM debrief: process vs outcome.
  - Company reveal only after reflection begins.
- [user] **XP rubric (100 per mission):**
  - Evidence quality 25, risk identification 25, acknowledging uncertainty 20, logic and consistency 20, reflection after reveal 10.
  - The AI explains each criterion from the player's text; a structured evaluator applies the caps.
  - Never reward profits, aggressive choices or lucky dice. Brier score across missions.
- [user] **MVP (p.6):**
  - Existing or new hero; Wizard/Fighter/Rogue.
  - One complete historical scenario.
  - DM narration + CommerzBuddy dialogue.
  - Free actions and 2–3 D20 checks.
  - Journal and rules-based consequences.
  - Decision + probability forecast.
  - Locked reveal, portfolio math and HP.
  - Debrief, XP and persistent learning history.
- [user] **Future (p.6):** live generation, branches and class trees, multiplayer, spells and gold economy, automatic anonymization, adaptive quests, long-term campaign mode.
- [user] **One-shot timing:**
  - 1–2 min: meet the company and pick a class.
  - 4–5 min: investigate (charts, NPCs, 2–3 checks).
  - 2 min: in-period news.
  - 2 min: commit.
  - 2–3 min: reveal, HP, debrief, XP.
- [user] **Protections:**
  - Before commitment, no future prices or identifying details for the DM or Buddy.
  - All numbers come from scenario data.
  - Failed rolls never hide learning material.
  - The €25 savings plan is optional and gives no XP.
  - Keep the prototype labelled; no personalised recommendations or promises of profit.
  - Mission complete = investigated, locked, viewed the outcome, got feedback, saved XP/journal.
