## Web research findings

- [web] ChartGame: hidden real S&P 500 chart, played day by day, name and period revealed at the end; scaling not disclosed. https://www.howthemarketworks.com/chartgame-com/
- [web] Ticker League "Spot the Stock" / Daily Challenge / Chart Challenge: hidden chart, the reveal shows the answer plus "the tell that identified it" and what others guessed. Recognising the stock is part of the game there, not a failure. https://tickerleague.com/games/spot-the-stock
- [web] Bloomberg trading game: unnamed chart, company revealed at the end. https://www.ngpf.org/blog/interactive/interactive-monday-stock-trading-game-aka-can-you-trade-individual-stocks/
- [web] k-anonymity and quasi-identifiers: attributes that are harmless alone but identifying in combination (ZIP + sex + birth date ≈ 87% of the US). Here the quasi-identifiers are event sequence, earnings timing, ratios and price shape. https://dataprivacylab.org/projects/identifiability
- [web] UK ICO "motivated intruder" test (March 2025): a competent intruder with internet access and generative AI; record results in a risk assessment and review them over time. Supports an LLM-attacker gate with results kept on record. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/anonymisation/how-do-we-ensure-anonymisation-is-effective
- [web] Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu, "The Memorization Problem": models recognise mega-caps from anonymized earnings calls (Apple, Meta, Microsoft 100%) and date WSJ headlines to the month 90% of the time. Recall is weaker for small stocks and collapses after the training cutoff. https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14765v2
- [web] Glasserman & Lin (2023): removing names helps, and the "distraction effect" is strongest for large firms. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17322
- [web] Look-Ahead-Bench: masking identifiers leaks, and heavy anonymization can destroy the signal. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24564
- [web] Staab et al., ICLR 2024: LLMs infer attributes from scrubbed text at ~85% top-1. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07298
- [web] "LLMs are Advanced Anonymizers": an adversary-then-rewrite loop, the closest prior art to our gate. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13846
- [web] Agentic web-search re-identification: up to 68% recall at 90% precision. https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16800
- [web] MiFID II: a personal recommendation counts as advice and can apply to apps. "Not investment advice" is necessary but not enough, and no personalised recommendations may be made on real instruments. https://www.esma.europa.eu/node/202977
- [web] ESMA (Feb 2024): educational communications should use clearly labelled historical data. ESMA also flags gamification that rewards trading volume as a risk. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-02/ESMA74-1103241886-912_Warnings_on_Social_Media_and_Investment_Recommendations.pdf
- [web] No comparable game discloses price scaling. Disclosing it would differentiate us.
