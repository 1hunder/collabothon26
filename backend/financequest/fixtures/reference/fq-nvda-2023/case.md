# NVIDIA Corporation (Nasdaq: NVDA): Historical Case Reference

Server-only source for the curated **easy** Guild notice. Real names appear only here and in the
manifest's `secret` block.

## 1. Purpose, scope, and learner objective

| Checkpoint | Date | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Intro (G0) | 2023-02-23 | Day after Q4 FY2023 results; ChatGPT boom under way |
| Shock (G1) | 2023-05-24 | Q1 FY2024 results and the $11bn Q2 outlook (after the close) |
| Decision (G2) | 2023-05-25 | Shares +24% in one session |
| Horizon (G3) | 2023-08-23 | Close before the Q2 FY2024 report |

**Why this window.** A clear, readable story with a forgiving outcome: the growing data-center line
is visible in the ledgers before the shock, the shock is a company forecast far above expectations,
and the price kept rising afterwards (decision close $379.80 → horizon close $471.16 as traded, about
+24%). The teaching question is not "is the company good" but "what is already in the price".

## 2. Evidence conventions and information checkpoints

US dollars in millions. Prices split-adjusted (10-for-1 split, 2024); the disguise rescales them.

- **G2:** the May 25 session (+24%).
- **After:** the Aug 23 Q2 report ($13.5bn revenue) and later prices, debrief only.

## 3. Company identity and business context (as known at G0)

Designer of GPUs for gaming, data centers (AI training and inference), professional visualisation and
automotive. ChatGPT (launched Nov 2022) triggered a rush to build generative-AI systems on NVIDIA's
A100/H100 data-center GPUs. CEO Jensen Huang; CFO Colette Kress; headquarters Santa Clara, California.

## 4. Public financial information available at G0

Q4 FY2023 (quarter to 2023-01-29): revenue 6,051 (-21% y/y); data center 3,616 (+11% y/y); gaming
1,831 (-46% y/y). Q1 FY2024 outlook: revenue 6,500 ±2%. FY2023: revenue 26,974; net income 4,368;
cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities 13,296; total debt about 10,950.

## 5. Announcements and news available at G0

The generative-AI boom and reports of large GPU orders from cloud providers and AI start-ups.

## 6. The shock: public information at G1

Q1 FY2024 (quarter to 2023-04-30), reported 2023-05-24: revenue 7,192 (+19% q/q, -13% y/y); data
center record 4,284 (+14% y/y, +18% q/q); gaming 2,240 (-38% y/y); GAAP net income 2,043. Q2 outlook
revenue 11,000 ±2% versus analyst consensus near 7,150-7,200. Shares rose about 24% on May 25 and
the market value approached $1 trillion.

## 7. Later historical events (after the decision; for the reveal and debrief only)

2023-08-23: Q2 FY2024 revenue 13,507 (data center 10,323), Q3 outlook 16,000. The stock had climbed
further into the report; the AI-chip boom continued well beyond the horizon.
