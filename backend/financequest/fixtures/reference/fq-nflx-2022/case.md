# Netflix, Inc. (Nasdaq: NFLX): Historical Case Reference

Server-only source for the curated **hard** Guild notice. Real names appear only here and in the
manifest's `secret` block.

## 1. Purpose, scope, and learner objective

| Checkpoint | Date | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Intro (G0) | 2022-01-21 | Day after the Q4 2021 letter; shares -22% on weak Q1 guidance |
| Shock (G1) | 2022-04-19 | Q1 2022 letter: first subscriber loss in over a decade |
| Decision (G2) | 2022-04-20 | Shares -35% in one session; Pershing Square exits |
| Horizon (G3) | 2022-07-19 | Close before the Q2 2022 report |

**Why this window.** The evidence genuinely points both ways: subscribers shrinking and growth
slowing for years, but revenue still growing, a 25% operating margin, positive free cash flow, and
new levers (paid sharing, an ad tier). After a 35% fall nobody could know whether it was a bargain or
a trap. Decision close $226.19 → horizon close $201.63 as traded (about -11%), with a deeper dip to
about $163 in May in between. Hard on purpose: surface signals mislead in both directions.

## 2. Evidence conventions and information checkpoints

US dollars in millions; members are counts. Prices split-adjusted; the disguise rescales them.
- **G0:** Q4 2021 letter (2022-01-20): revenue 7,709; paid net adds 8.28M; paid memberships 221.84M;
  Q1 2022 guidance +2.5M (versus 3.98M in Q1 2021); US/Canada price increase (Jan 2022).
- **G1:** Q1 2022 letter (2022-04-19): net loss of 200k members (guided +2.5M); Russia exit cost
  ~700k; revenue 7,868 (+9.8% y/y); operating margin 25.1%; Q2 guide -2M; 100M+ sharing households
  (30M+ in UCAN); cash ~6.0bn; gross debt ~14.6bn; free cash flow ~802; ads tier under consideration.
- **G2:** the April 20 session (-35%) and Pershing Square's exit.
- **After:** May lows, the Q2 2022 report (-970k, better than feared), the ad tier launch in Nov 2022.

## 3. Company identity and business context (as known at G0)

The world's largest subscription streaming service, headquartered in Los Gatos, California; co-CEOs
Reed Hastings and Ted Sarandos. Competition from Disney+, HBO Max, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+.
Large multi-year content obligations.

## 4. Public financial information available at G0

See section 2 (Q4 2021 letter). Paid net additions had slowed each year since the 2020 pandemic boost.

## 5. Announcements and news available at G0

US/Canada price increase; the Jan 21 share drop of about 22% on the weak Q1 guidance.

## 6. The shock: public information at G1

Q1 2022: first paid-membership decline in more than ten years (-200k), Q2 guidance of -2M, revenue
growth slowing to 9.8%, and management citing account sharing (100M+ households) and competition.

## 7. Later historical events (after the decision; for the reveal and debrief only)

- 2022-04-20: Pershing Square (Bill Ackman) sold its entire stake at a loss.
- 2022-05-11: close about $163.
- 2022-07-19: Q2 2022 loss of 970k members, better than the 2M forecast; shares rose the next day.
- 2022-11: ad-supported tier launched; 2023: paid sharing rolled out and growth resumed.
