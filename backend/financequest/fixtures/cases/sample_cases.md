# Sample source cases (fixture)

Three small cases for the dry run. Prices are synthetic fixture series, not market data.

## Boeing 2024

Lesson: a safety crisis at a famous manufacturer can erase years of margin and trust at once.

- 2024-01-05: A door panel detaches from a new jet in flight; regulators ground the model. Boeing says it will review quality control.
- 2024-03-25: The chief executive announces he will step down by year end. Boeing reports a $4 billion cash burn guide for the year.
- 2024-07-31: Boeing posts a second-quarter loss and a 10% workforce reduction plan.

## Marrow Bay Brewing

Lesson: a small brewer that grows faster than its cash flow can finance ends up selling equity at a low price.

- 2021-03-02: Marrow Bay Brewing opens a second plant for $4.2 million, funded by short-term bank debt.
- 2021-06-15: Sales rise 35% but operating cash flow stays negative; the brewer draws its credit line.
- 2021-10-04: Marrow Bay Brewing sells new shares at a 20% discount to repay the credit line.

## Tidewell Ferries

Lesson: a ferry operator with one reported event cannot teach a sequence.

- 2022-05-10: Tidewell Ferries loses its main route contract.
